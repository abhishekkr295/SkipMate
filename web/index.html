Privacy Policy

Notice: This is a standard transparency document for SkipMate. Review with legal counsel prior to commercial store distribution.

1. Commitment to Privacy

SkipMate is designed to function strictly locally on your Android device. The core automatic skip assistant does not inspect, read, or transmit your browsing activity, video history, or personal data.

2. Android Accessibility Service Usage

SkipMate requests the Android Accessibility Service permission solely to inspect user interface nodes in the official YouTube application for the presence of the standard "Skip Ads" control and trigger a simulated tap. No text entered on keyboards or private data is captured, logged, or uploaded.

3. Firebase Authentication & Firestore

If you create an optional account to manage SkipMate Pro subscriptions, your email, name, and subscription tier are securely processed through Google Firebase. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
